=== USER ===
Two input images: a scene in which a hand holds a small {{ name }}, and a studio photo of the exact {{ name }} to use. In the scene, replace the small {{ name }} in the hand with the one from the studio photo: keep its shape, squared cap, glass, liquid colour and finish exactly as in the studio photo, completely wordless (no label, letters or logo). Keep the scene's size for it: small in the hand, at the same position and angle, with the fingers wrapped around it exactly as before. Change nothing else in the scene: same person, clothing, framing, light and background.
